Information We Collect

I may collect information that you voluntarily provide when you contact me or submit an enquiry through this website.

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number, if provided
  • Project details or messages
  • Any other information you choose to share

02

How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries and messages
  • Understand your project requirements
  • Provide quotations or service information
  • Communicate about potential or ongoing projects
  • Improve website performance and user experience
  • Maintain website security and prevent misuse

Your information will not be used for unrelated purposes without informing you where required.

Contact Forms

When you submit a contact form, your information may be processed through a third-party form service, such as Formspree, which securely delivers your message to me.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information, passwords, payment details, or confidential documents through the contact form.

Information Sharing

I do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Your information may be shared only when necessary with:

Third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

This website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve functionality, understand website usage, or maintain security.

If analytics or non-essential cookies are used, you may be asked to provide consent where required. You can also manage or disable cookies through your browser settings.

Data Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to:

When information is no longer needed, it will be deleted or securely disposed of where reasonably practicable.
